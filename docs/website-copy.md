**Body:**
"Tested" is a word that gets used loosely in this industry. For us it means a
sample from the batch you are buying is sent to an independent analytical

**Copy rule for product descriptions:** describe the *material*, never an
*outcome*. Chemical identity, purity, form, solubility, and storage are all
fine. Anything describing what a compound does in a body is not — it converts
your listing into an unapproved drug claim.

## 4. Compliance and policy pages

These need input from a lawyer familiar with your jurisdiction and your
payment processor's rules. The outlines below are a drafting starting point,
not legal advice, and should not be published as-is.

### Terms of sale — cover
Eligibility and minimum age; research-use-only restriction and buyer
warranty; order acceptance and pricing; payment terms; shipping and risk of
loss; returns and refunds; limitation of liability; indemnity; governing law;
prohibited uses including resale for human consumption.

### Shipping policy — cover
Processing times and cut-off; carriers and service levels; tracking;
destinations served and any excluded regions; customs responsibility for
international orders; handling of lost or damaged shipments; cold-chain
handling where applicable.

### Returns policy — cover
Return window; condition requirements (typically unopened and seals intact for
research materials); non-returnable categories; the process for incorrect or
damaged items; refund timing and method; who pays return shipping in each case.

### Privacy policy — cover
Data collected and why; cookies and analytics; payment processing and third
parties; retention periods; user rights and how to exercise them; contact
details for privacy enquiries.
