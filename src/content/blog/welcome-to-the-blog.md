This is the first post on our new site. It exists partly to say hello and
partly to set expectations about what will appear here.

## What we plan to publish

Three kinds of writing, roughly:

- **Methods and protocols** — procedures written out properly, including the
  steps that usually get skipped because they seem obvious to whoever wrote
  them down.
- **Explainers** — background pieces for readers who are curious but not
  specialists, with sources linked so you can verify the claims yourself.
- **Laboratory notes** — shorter observations from work in progress. Not
  everything here will be a finished conclusion.

## How we intend to write

Plainly, and with the reasoning left in. A result without its method is
difficult to trust and impossible to reproduce, so we would rather publish one
thorough piece than five thin ones.

We will also be explicit about uncertainty. If something is an educated guess,
it will be labelled as an educated guess rather than presented as settled.

## Following along

Every article is published to our [RSS feed](/rss.xml) as soon as it goes
live. There is no newsletter and no tracking — subscribe with whatever reader
you already use.

Corrections and suggestions are genuinely welcome. Our
[contact page](/contact/) has the details.
