RAD-140 (Testolone)

A non-steroidal SARM whose published record is preclinical, with notably less human data than MK-2866.

Written by: Research Lab USA Editorial Team Published: August 20, 2026 Last updated: September 23, 2026

What it is

RAD-140 is a non-steroidal selective androgen receptor modulator investigated in preclinical work, including studies in animal models of muscle wasting and, separately, hormone-receptor-positive breast cancer.

State of the evidence

The published record is thinner than for MK-2866 and largely preclinical. Case reports of liver injury associated with products sold as RAD-140 exist in the literature; because those products were not independently characterized, whether the compound or a contaminant was responsible generally cannot be established.

That ambiguity is itself the point. Without analytical characterisation you cannot attribute an outcome to a compound at all.

Regulatory status

Not approved for human or veterinary use anywhere we are aware of, and prohibited in sport under the WADA code.

Handling

Confirm form, solubility and storage against your certificate of analysis before preparing anything.
