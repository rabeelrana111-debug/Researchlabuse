Privacy Policy

What we collect, why, and how long we keep it. Short, because this site collects very little.

Awaiting legal review. This page describes our current practice accurately, but it has not yet been reviewed by a lawyer. If you need a binding answer about how your information is handled before you send it, write to info@researchlabusa.com and ask.

What we collect

Only what you type into our contact form: your name, your email address, a subject line and your message. Every field is one you fill in yourself — we do not collect anything about you in the background.

Like every web server, ours records requests it receives, including IP addresses, in standard server logs kept by our hosting provider. Those logs exist for security and troubleshooting. We do not use them to build a profile of you.

What we do not do

  • We set no cookies and run no analytics or tracking scripts. You can confirm this: open your browser’s developer tools on any page of this site and look at the Application and Network tabs.
  • We have no user accounts and no shopping cart, so there is no password or payment information to lose.
  • We do not sell your information, and we do not share it with anyone for their own marketing.

Why we use it

To read your message and reply to it. That is the only purpose. We will not add you to a mailing list because you asked a question.

Who else sees it

Our web host processes the form submission and carries the resulting email, and our email provider stores it. They act as service providers on our behalf and are not free to use your message for their own purposes.

Pages in our peptides section link to Avid Peptides (opens in a new tab), our peptide supply partner. Following that link takes you to their website, which has its own privacy policy and is not covered by this one. We do not pass them your contact details.

How long we keep it

Contact-form emails are kept while a conversation is open and for up to two years afterwards, so we can pick up an earlier thread. After that they are deleted. Ask us to delete yours sooner and we will.

Your choices

Write to info@researchlabusa.com to ask what we hold about you, to have it corrected, or to have it deleted. We will respond within 30 days. Depending on where you live you may have further statutory rights; tell us what you need and we will work out whether it applies.

Children

This site publishes laboratory reference material and is not directed to children. We do not knowingly collect information from anyone under 16.

Changes

If this policy changes materially, the revised version will appear here with a new date at the top of the page.

Contact

Questions about this policy go to info@researchlabusa.com.
