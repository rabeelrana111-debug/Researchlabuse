Cyclazodone

A substituted aminorex derivative with a notably thin published record.

Written by: Research Lab USA Editorial Team Published: August 20, 2026 Last updated: September 28, 2026

What it is

Cyclazodone is a derivative of aminorex, related structurally to pemoline. It belongs to a family of stimulant compounds, several of which were withdrawn from medical use.

State of the evidence

The published record is very thin. There is little peer-reviewed pharmacology, essentially no controlled human data, and no established safety profile.

We state that plainly because an absence of evidence is the most useful thing we can tell you about this compound. Related compounds in the same family have documented hepatotoxicity, which is a reason for caution in interpreting the silence rather than comfort.

Handling

Verify identity and purity analytically. For compounds this poorly characterised, a certificate of analysis is the only meaningful evidence of what you actually have.
