Flmodafinil

A fluorinated modafinil analogue, also written CRL-40,940 and bisfluoromodafinil.

Written by: Research Lab USA Editorial Team Published: August 20, 2026 Last updated: September 28, 2026

What it is

Flmodafinil is a modafinil analogue carrying fluorine substitutions on the aromatic rings. It appears in the literature and in product listings under several names — CRL-40,940, bisfluoromodafinil, lauflumide — which makes searching for it unusually awkward.

State of the evidence

Published pharmacology is limited. Claims about how it compares to modafinil circulate widely but rest on very little published work, and structural similarity is not evidence of comparable behavior.

Naming and identity

Because several names are in use, verify identity by CAS number and analytical data rather than by the name on the label. This is the compound most likely in this catalog to arrive as something other than what was ordered.

Handling

Supplied as a powder. Confirm purity and storage conditions against your certificate of analysis.
