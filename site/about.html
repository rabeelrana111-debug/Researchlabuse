About

Who we are and how we work

We publish reference material on research compounds for people who read the methods section first.

Why we publish

Most writing about research compounds falls into one of two camps: too thin to act on, or written to rank in search rather than to inform. We started publishing to fill the gap in between — material detailed enough to be useful, written plainly enough to be read.

How we write

Every guide states what is known, where that knowledge comes from, and what remains unresolved. Claims are linked to the study behind them so you can check the original rather than take our word for it.

Where the evidence is thin or contested, we say so. Rounding an open question up to a confident answer is the most common failure in this subject area, and it is the one we work hardest to avoid.

Corrections

When something turns out to be wrong, the page is updated with a dated note describing what changed and why. We do not quietly edit pages and leave no trace — if you relied on an earlier version, you deserve to know it changed.

What we do not do

We do not provide dosing guidance, protocols for human use, or advice on obtaining materials for personal use. Everything here is written for laboratory research by qualified professionals, and we cannot answer questions that fall outside that.

For laboratory and research use only. The materials discussed on this website are not medicines, dietary supplements, cosmetics or food. None has been evaluated or approved by the FDA for human or veterinary use. Nothing here is medical advice, and nothing here should be read as a suggestion that any compound is safe or effective for any purpose.
