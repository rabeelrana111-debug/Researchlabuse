Topic

Research peptides

Sequences, reconstitution, cold-chain handling and stability — the practical details that determine whether a result is reproducible.

What this section covers

Peptides are unforgiving materials to work with. Reconstitution solvent, temperature history and freeze-thaw cycles all affect what is actually in the vial by the time it reaches an assay, and those details are routinely left out of published protocols.

Our guides give the sequence, molecular weight and purity method for each peptide, then set out reconstitution and storage in full — including the steps that seem obvious to whoever wrote them down.

Lyophilized material and material in solution behave differently, and we treat them separately rather than collapsing both into one instruction.

What we cover

In our peptide guides

Sequence and identity

Amino acid sequence, molecular weight, and the analytical method used to establish purity.

Reconstitution

Appropriate solvents, concentrations, and the handling steps that affect stability after reconstitution.

Cold chain

Storage temperatures for lyophilized and reconstituted material, and what freeze-thaw cycling costs you.

What has been studied, in what model, and how far the findings have been replicated.
