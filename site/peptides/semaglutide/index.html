Semaglutide

A GLP-1 receptor agonist. Unlike most compounds cataloged here, approved medicines containing it exist.

Written by: Research Lab USA Editorial Team Published: August 20, 2026 Last updated: September 28, 2026

What it is

Semaglutide is a glucagon-like peptide-1 receptor agonist: a modified peptide engineered for a substantially longer half-life than native GLP-1.

An important distinction

Approved prescription medicines containing semaglutide exist, which makes this page different from the others here. Those medicines are regulated products with established manufacturing, and material sold as a research chemical is not equivalent to them in any respect that matters — not purity, not sterility, not fill accuracy.

Research-grade material is not a substitute for a prescribed medicine, and nothing on this page should be read as suggesting otherwise. Compounded and grey-market semaglutide has been the subject of repeated regulatory warnings, including dosing errors traced to unlabelled concentration differences.

Handling and stability

Peptides of this size are sensitive to temperature and to agitation. Follow the storage conditions on your certificate of analysis, and treat cold-chain excursions as affecting the specification rather than as a formality.
