  • MK-2866 (Ostarine) The largest published clinical record of any SARM

Peptides

  • BPC-157 Pentadecapeptide; almost no published human data

  • TB-500 Thymosin beta-4 fragment; supplied lyophilized

Nootropics

  • Adrafinil Prodrug of modafinil; withdrawn from sale
  • Cyclazodone Aminorex derivative; very little published
  • Flmodafinil Bisfluoromodafinil; also called CRL-40,940
  • Phenylpiracetam Phenylated piracetam analogue
